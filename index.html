Paper & Reel

Conversion bench

Six stations for reshaping documents and footage: split, build, merge, and re-encode — all on this page.

Nothing leaves your device. Every conversion — PDFs, images, and video — runs locally in this browser tab using WebAssembly. Large video jobs can take a while and use noticeable memory since there's no server doing the encoding.

Output

Paper & Reel converts PDFs with pdf.js & pdf-lib, and video with a WebAssembly build of FFmpeg. Very large files may exceed what the browser tab can hold in memory.
